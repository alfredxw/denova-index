# Quiet mystery prose

Keep the camera close to a character with a concrete task. Choose two physical details that affect the task; omit decorative inventory. Use precise verbs and short dialogue with a purpose. Let the reader infer emotion from timing, gestures and omissions.

A useful scene rhythm: ordinary task → discrepant detail → plausible explanation → evidence that narrows the choices. Alternate sentence length without a rigid formula. Never announce that a room is "mysterious" or that something "changes everything."

Example: The return slot was empty. Mara checked the date on her receipt, then the scratch beside the lock. Someone had oiled the hinge since yesterday.

Avoid repeating an atmospheric image, adding a twist without planted evidence, or treating a narrator's guess as settled truth. End with enough concrete information for the next action.
